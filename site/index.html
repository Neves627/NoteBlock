Sobre & Intuito

NoteBlock was created to replace the loose papers in the desk drawer with quick, lightweight, and always visible digital notes on Windows — without distractions or mandatory accounts.

Always Visible

Keep your notes always on top of any window to never lose focus on your tasks.

Automatic Updates

Whenever there are updates or improvements, the application updates itself when opened.

Lightweight & Customizable

Switch between light and dark themes and automatically save the size of each note.

Built-in Alarms

Set custom alarms and reminders directly on your notes so you never miss a deadline.

Paste Images & Draw

Paste images straight from your clipboard and sketch or annotate freely on any note.
